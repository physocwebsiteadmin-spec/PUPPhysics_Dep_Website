PUP Bachelor of Science in Physics
The Bachelor of Science in Physics of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines is a rigorous undergraduate program designed to provide students with a strong foundation in fundamental physics, mathematical methods, experimental techniques, and scientific inquiry. The program emphasizes the understanding of the laws of the universe governing energy, matter, space, and time, while fostering analytical thinking, innovation, and ethical scientific practice. Established in 1998-1999, the PUP BS Physics program has undergone several curriculum revisions to remain responsive to advancements in science, technology, and industry needs. It is currently housed under the Department of Physical Science in the College of Science and is delivered through inquiry-based and outcome-oriented learning. The PUP BS physics curriculum integrates:
  • Classical and Modern Physics
  • Advance Mathematics
  • Laboratory and Instrumentation training
  • Computational Analysis
  • Research, Collaboration, and Scientific Communication
Students are encouraged to explore specialized topics with the help of research groups in material science, quantum mechanics, condensed matter physics, general relativity, atmospheric physics, mathematical physics, observational astrophysics, and medical physics. While developing 21st century competencies including critical thinking, leadership, adaptability, and lifelong learning. The program prepares its graduates for professional endeavors, research, and advanced studies, while instilling a strong sense of social responsibility, scientific integrity, and service to the community that aligned with the mission and vision of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines.
BS Physics Career Options Graduates of the BS Physics program acquired highly transferable skills in quantitative analysis, modeling, experimentation, and problem solving. These skills enable its graduates to pursue diverse career paths in both laboratory and non laboratory settings. 1. Research Development Physics graduates may pursue careers in theoretical, experimental, or applied research, contributing to scientific discovery, innovation, and technology development in universities, research institutions, and laboratories. 2. Graduate Studies The program provides strong preparation for advanced studies such as master's and doctoral degrees in physics, engineering, data science, medical physics, and other interdisciplinary fields. 3. Applied Industry Graduates can work in physics-driven industries such as manufacturing, electronics, semiconductors, and energy, applying principles of physics to instrumentation, quality control, product testing, and process optimization. 4. Data Science With strong training in mathematics and analytical thinking, BS Physics graduates are well-suited for data science roles involving data analysis, modeling, simulation, and computational problem-solving across various sectors. 5. Medical Physics Graduates may enter the healthcare sector as medical physics professionals, supporting radiation therapy, diagnostic imaging, and radiation safety, typically with additional training or graduate studies. 6. Earth Science Physics graduates can specialize in geophysics, meteorology, atmospheric science, or environmental physics, working on climate analysis, natural hazard assessment, and Earth system research. 7. Space Science Careers in astrophysics and space science involve the study of celestial objects and cosmic phenomena, often pursued through research institutions or advanced academic training. 8. Science Education Graduates may become physics educators or academic instructors, teaching at secondary or tertiary levels and promoting scientific literacy, critical thinking, and inquiry-based learning. 9. Science Communication Graduates can pursue roles in science writing, technical communication, or policy support, translating complex scientific concepts into clear, accessible information for diverse audiences. 10. Technical Consulting Physics graduates may work as technical consultants or analysts, providing expert problem-solving, modeling, and evidence-based solutions in science-oriented organizations.
