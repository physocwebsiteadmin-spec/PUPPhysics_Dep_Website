Organization and Program Level Achievements


Establishment & Expansion of Specialized Research Groups (2023–2025)

Research Groups and Specializations:

  • Quantum Mechanics Group – quantum measurement, decoherence, entanglement, teleportation
  • Material Science Group – synthesis, characterization, computational materials physics
  • Condensed Matter Physics Group – semiconductor device physics, DFT, SiC materials
  • Gravitational Physics Group – general relativity and cosmic phenomena
  • Atmospheric Physics Group – meteorology, climate physics, atmospheric analysis (with PAGASA official)
  • Computational Physics Group – optics, radiation science, complex systems modeling
  • Mathematical Physics Group – stochastic systems, fractional systems modeling
  • Astrophysics Group – astronomy and astrophysics research with PhilSA and Max Planck mentors

Major Academic Output: 77 Undergraduate Theses Successfully Defended (2021–2025)

From AY 2021–2022 to AY 2024–2025, the program recorded 77 defended undergraduate theses, covering advanced fields such as:

  • Quantum Teleportation
  • Laser-Material Interactions
  • Renewable Energy Systems
  • Radiation Shielding
  • Meteorological Modeling
  • Semiconductor Physics
  • Computational Materials Science

Excellence in Community Extension: SKTK – Liknayan Para sa Bayan (2022–2025)

A major program-level achievement is the successful 3-year implementation of the SKTK–LPB Extension Project, designed to enhance physics education in public schools.

Phase 1 (2022–2023): Online Capability Building (Zoom)

  • Focus: fundamental physics (Newton’s Laws, Work, Energy, Fluids)
  • 7 faculty and 11 Physics Society volunteers facilitated
  • 15 DepEd-San Juan teachers participated
  • Received an overall “Excellent” rating

Phase 2 (2023–2024): Hands-On Laboratory Training (On-site)

  • Conducted Jan 2024 at San Juan National High School
  • 13 Physics Society student facilitators, faculty experts
  • 18 teacher participants
  • Experiments: free fall, inclined plane, momentum, geometric optics
  • Evaluation scores ranged 4.82–4.94 (Excellent)

Extension Program Highlights (2022–2024):

  • Rigorous needs assessment and co-development with DepEd
  • Comprehensive lecture series and experimental workshops
  • Systematic evaluation and feedback analytics
  • Strong inter-institutional partnership and sustainability plans
