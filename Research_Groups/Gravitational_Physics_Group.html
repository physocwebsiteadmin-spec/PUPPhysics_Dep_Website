Our universe can be described by two main theories: Quantum Mechanics, which deals with the physics of subatomic particles, and the General Theory of Relativity, a theory of gravity formulated by Albert Einstein in 1915 that describes large-scale structures such as stars and galaxies, and gravitational phenomena are the result of these stuffs moving in spacetime. General Relativity has made numerous predictions, from the existence of black holes to the deflection of light by gravity. Its success is reflected in its far-reaching applications in astrophysics and cosmology, such as the detection of dark matter through gravitational lensing and our current model of the origin of the universe.
The PUP Gravitational Physics Group, founded in 2023, aims to contribute to the ongoing endeavor of understanding the universe. The group explores the following fascinating topics:
  • Dark matter
  • Binary systems and Extreme-mass ratio inspirals (EMRIs)
  • Black holes and wormholes
  • Self-force
  • Warp drive
  • Doubly special relativity
  • Black-hole analog models
  • Anisotropic stars
  • Gravitational waves
  • Alternative theories of gravity
and many others like combination and quantum mechanics, general relativity, and theoretical astrophysics. If any of these topics pique your interest, you are welcome to join our group!
