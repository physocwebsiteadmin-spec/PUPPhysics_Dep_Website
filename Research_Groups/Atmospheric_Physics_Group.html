The Atmospheric Physics Research Group of the PUP Bachelor of Science in Physics program is an academic and scientific initiative dedicated to the study of atmospheric processes through the principles of physics. Established in response to the growing challenges posed by climate change, extreme weather events, urban pollution, and environmental degradation, the group serves as a platform for students and faculty to engage in research on atmospheric dynamics, cloud microphysics, radiation balance, air quality, climate variability, and remote sensing. Through theoretical analysis, computational modeling, and data-driven approaches, the group aims to strengthen technical and analytical competencies essential for modern atmospheric science.
Anchored in PUP’s mission of research excellence and social responsibility, the group contributes to national development by aligning its research with disaster risk reduction, climate adaptation, and environmental sustainability initiatives. It supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals in particular Climate Action and Sustainable Cities that fosters collaboration with government agencies such as PAGASA, DOST, and DENR. By bridging fundamental physics with real-world applications, the Atmospheric Physics Research Group aspires to develop environmentally conscious, research-oriented physicists capable of addressing pressing atmospheric and climate-related challenges in the Philippines.
