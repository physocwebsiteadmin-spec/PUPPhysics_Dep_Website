The Condensed Matter Physics Group is dedicated to advancing the understanding of condensed matter physics through the integrated pursuit of theoretical, computational, and experimental inquiry. Guided by a vision of cultivating an innovative research environment, the Group brings together aspiring physicists committed to exploring the fundamental properties and behaviors of matter in its condensed states.

By fostering close collaboration across its three core methodological pillars — theory, computation, and experiment — the Group seeks to bridge disciplinary boundaries and develop a cohesive, rigorous approach to contemporary problems in condensed matter physics. Equally central to its purpose is the cultivation of a supportive and intellectually engaged community, where emerging researchers are encouraged to develop their capabilities and contribute to the broader advancement of the field.
Through this dual commitment to scientific excellence and community building, the Condensed Matter Physics Group aspires to serve as a productive and dynamic hub for physics research and scholarly development.
