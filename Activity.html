Since the academic year 2020 – 2021, the PUP Physics Society has conducted a sustained program of skills-oriented workshops aimed at fostering the academic and professional development of its members. Its annual programming workshop series has progressively expanded from foundational Python instruction to comprehensive, multi-day events covering scientific computing, data analysis, symbolic mathematics, and technical documentation. The Society has further complemented these with physics seminars featuring distinguished alumni and industry practitioners, offering students informed guidance on career pathways within the field. These activities collectively reflect the Society's commitment to developing technically proficient and professionally prepared physics graduates.
❮ ❯
The PUP Physics Society fulfills its social responsibility through science education outreach programs designed to extend quality physics instruction to secondary-level students in partner communities. In collaboration with the School Division Office of San Juan City and San Juan National High School, Society members serve as volunteer educators, facilitating sessions that promote scientific literacy and critical thinking at the pre-collegiate level. This initiative affirms the Society's commitment to community engagement and its belief that the dissemination of scientific knowledge is a meaningful contribution to national development.
