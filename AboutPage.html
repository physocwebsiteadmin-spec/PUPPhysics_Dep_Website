PUP Department of Physics

The Bachelor of Science in Physics program of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines started under the Department of Natural Sciences in 2007, alongside the Bachelor of Science in Biology and Bachelor of Science in Chemistry. The department was initially led by its first chairperson, Prof. Elnora Serrano. In 2010, the BS Physics program became part of the Department of Physical Sciences, together with BS Chemistry, under the leadership of its first chairperson, Dr. Edgardo A. Latoza.

After 16 years of growth and development, in 2026, the BS Physics program has entered a new chapter with the establishment of the Department of Physics. The newly established department is led by its first chairperson, Dr. John Paul A. Besagas, marking another significant milestone in the continuing development of the BS Physics program. This new milestone begins its growth through the approval of funding for the Institution Development Program (IDP) project, “Establishment of Quantum Science and Quantum Materials Laboratory to Strengthen the BS Physics Program and Research Capability of the Polytechnic University of the Philippines,” proposed by Dr. John Paul A. Besagas and Dr. Rengie Mark D. Mailig.

As the department also begins its new chapter, the PUP BS Physics program continues to emphasize the significance and value of research involvement by recognizing outstanding research achievements through initiatives, such as the yearly BS Physics Research Colloquium and recognition of the students who have completed their undergraduate thesis, students who have received awards in the research colloquium, and students who have participated as a presented in the yearly Samahang Pisika ng Pilipinas (SPP) Physics Conference.
