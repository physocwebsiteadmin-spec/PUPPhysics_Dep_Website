MISSION

To revitalize the Physics Society (PhySoc) by fostering a vibrant and inclusive community that not only reconnects students within the Physics program but also bridges connections with peers across the College of Science. We are committed to helping the nation, especially the science and technology sector, by promoting well-trained and dedicated Filipino scientists and technologists. We also aim to nurture a responsible, creative, and innovative citizenry, while promoting awareness and boosting the morale of all students of the Bachelor of Science in Physics and the wider studentry. Through these efforts, we strive to ensure that every student feels valued, empowered, and represented.

VISION

A vibrant and interconnected Physics Society recognized for fostering an active, engaged community and championing student welfare. We envision ourselves as a leading voice in addressing academic challenges, advancing academic excellence, and contributing to national progress in science and technology through the development of competent Filipino scientists and technologists. By cultivating innovation, responsibility, and collaboration, and through meaningful partnerships and an expanded network, we aim to strengthen our community and create a lasting, positive impact within and beyond the College of Science.

History

The PUP Physics Society (PhySoc) was established in 1998, alongside the introduction of the Bachelor of Science in Physics program(BS Physics). Since its founding, the organization has been dedicated to promoting academic excellence, scientific inquiry, research, and the professional development of physics students through various academic, extension, and student-centered activities that foster collaboration and strengthen the university's physics community.

In its early years, the society operated under the supervision of the Department of Physical Sciences within the College of Science. Following the establishment of the Department of Physics, the organization has since been affiliated with and operates under this department within the College of Science. The PUP Physics Society continues to serve as the official academic organization of BS Physics students and remains an officially recognized and accredited student organization of the university.
