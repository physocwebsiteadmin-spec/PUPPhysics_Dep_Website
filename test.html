Committees

OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT - Executive Committee (EXEC COMM)

The Executive Committee is responsible for leading the strategic planning and overall direction of the society. It spearheads decision-making processes, oversees the implementation of initiatives, and ensures alignment with society's goals and values. This committee acts as the central governing body, guiding the society's actions and maintaining organizational coherence.

OFFICE OF THE VICE PRESIDENT - Internal Affairs Committee (IAC)

Under the Office of the Vice President, the Internal Affairs Committee is responsible for fostering internal unity, effective communication, and coordination within the society. It oversees member welfare, facilitates internal engagement activities, and ensures smooth collaboration across all departments. In addition, the committee plays a key role in managing the Membership and Program Committee, handling matters related to membership development, participation, and the planning and execution of internal programs.

OFFICE OF THE SECRETARY - Secretariat Committee (SEC COMM)

The Secretariat Committee, under the Office of the Secretary, is responsible for the documentation, communication, and administrative support of the society. It manages official records, meeting minutes, correspondence, and internal communications to ensure transparency and organizational efficiency. The committee also oversees the preparation and safekeeping of documents, maintains databases, and supports logistical needs for meetings and events. As the society's central administrative arm, SEC COMM ensures smooth information flow and operational coordination across all committees.

OFFICE OF THE FINANCE CHAIRPERSON - Finance and Resource Management Committee (FRMC)

The Finance and Resource Management Committee is responsible for overseeing the financial health and resource allocation of the society. It manages budgeting, accounting. and financial reporting to ensure transparency and accountability. The committee is also in charge of generating, monitoring, and optimizing the use of funds and resources, including sponsorships, fundraising activities, and logistical support. FRMC plays a critical role in sustaining the society's operations through efficient financial planning and responsible resource management.

OFFICE OF THE AUDITOR CHAIRPERSON - Budget and Resource Allocation Committee (BRAC)

The Budget and Resource Allocation Committee is responsible for ensuring the proper and equitable distribution of the society's financial and material resources, It reviews, evaluates, and audits budget proposals, monitors expenditures, and ensures compliance with financial policies and procedures. BRAC serves as an internal check-and-batance mechanism by promoting transparency, accountability, and fiscal responsibility in all financial transactions and resource allocations within the organization.

OFFICE OF THE RESEARCH, EXTENSION, AND DEVELOPMENT CHAIRPERSON - Research and Technical Operations Committee (RTOC)

The Research and Technical Operations Committee is responsible for overseeing the society's initiatives in research, extension, development, and technical operations. The committee facilitates the planning, execution, and evaluation of research projects. community outreach programs and developmental activities. It also manages the technical aspects related to these initiatives, ensuring that resources and expertise are effectively utilize

OFFICE OF THE STUDENT SERVICES CHAIRPERSON - Student Interface and Support Services Committee (SISC)

The Student Interface and Support Services Committee is dedicated to addressing the needs and welfare of the student body. The committee oversees student affairs, services. and ushering, ensuring that members receive appropriate support and assistance throughout their involvement in the organization.

OFFICE OF THE ACADEMIC AFFAIRS CHAIRPERSON - Academic Planning and Logistics Committee (APLC)

The Academic Planning & Logistics Committee, under the Office of the Academic Affairs Chairperson, is responsible for organizing and managing the society's academic initiatives and ensuring the smooth execution of all related logistical needs. The committee oversees academic affairs, including educational programs, workshops, and academic support activities, while also handling logistics such as scheduling, venue coordination, and resource preparation.

OFFICE OF THE INTERNAL PUBLICITY AND COMMUNICATION CHAIRPERSON - Creative Innovations and Affairs Committee (CIAC)

The Creative Innovations and Affairs Committee is responsible for managing the society's internal publicity, creative content. and communication strategies, The committee leads the production of visual and written materials for internal campaigns, announcements, and events. It oversees publication, creatives, and documentation, ensuring that all materials reflect the organization's identity and values.

OFFICE OF THE EXTERNAL PUBLICITY AND COMMUNICATION CHAIRPERSON - Strategic Alliances Committee (SAC)

The Strategic Alliances Committee is responsible for managing the society's external relations and expanding its network of collaborators and supporters. The committee spearheads initiatives related to external affairs, sponsorship acquisition, and partnership development. ensuring the organization builds and maintains strong connections with external stakeholders. SAC also represents the society in external engagements and collaborates with public and private entities to create meaningful and strategic alliances that support the organization's goals and programs.
